About Me

Artist statement

I am Venkata, a multidisciplinary artist focused on abstract storytelling. My practice blends layered textures, calligraphic marks, and field notes from daily life.

Through mixed media works, I explore the tension between quiet landscapes and urban movement, creating pieces that invite slow, reflective viewing.
